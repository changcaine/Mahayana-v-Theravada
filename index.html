Comparison of Greater Vehicle (Mahayana) and Lesser Vehicle (Theravāda)

Aspect

Greater Vehicle (Mahayana)

Lesser Vehicle (Theravāda)

1. Name Meaning

"Great Vehicle" – broad path for all beings

"Lesser Vehicle" (Mahayana term); Theravāda = "Teaching of the Elders"

2. Geographic Spread

East Asia: China, Japan, Korea, Vietnam, Tibet

South/Southeast Asia: Sri Lanka, Thailand, Myanmar, Laos, Cambodia

3. Scriptural Language

Sanskrit

Pāli

4. Scriptures

Mahayana Sutras (e.g., Lotus, Heart)

Pāli Canon (Tipiṭaka)

5. Ideal Figure

Bodhisattva – enlightenment for all

Arhat – personal liberation (Nibbāna)

6. Goal of Practice

Universal salvation; become a Buddha

Individual liberation; become an Arhat

7. View of the Buddha

Eternal, cosmic Buddha; many Buddhas exist

Historical Buddha (Siddhartha Gautama)

8. Philosophical Focus

Emptiness (Śūnyatā), Mind-only, Middle Way

Four Noble Truths, Eightfold Path, Dependent Origination

9. Monasticism

Important but flexible; more lay participation

Central role of monastic life and strict discipline

10. Ethical Emphasis

Compassion (karuṇā) and wisdom

Wisdom (paññā) and discipline

11. Rituals & Deities

Rich rituals; celestial Buddhas and Bodhisattvas

Minimal ritual; fewer supernatural elements

12. Meditation Styles

Includes Zen, Pure Land, Vajrayāna

Vipassanā (insight), Samatha (concentration)

13. Enlightenment View

Can be sudden or gradual; possible in this life

Gradual process over many lives

14. View of Other Paths

Inclusive; often sees Theravāda as foundational

More conservative; may view Mahayana as later development
